Finds - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Overview

Finds is a personal metadata collection app designed to help you catalog and locate items you've found. This privacy policy explains how we handle data in compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and data privacy regulations.

Key principle: Your data is stored exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal information with external services.

Data Collection and Usage

Precise Location Data

Data collected: GPS coordinates of locations where you find items

Purpose: To record the geographic location of finds and display them on an interactive map within the app

Linked to identity: No. Location data is stored locally on your device and is not associated with any user account or personal identifier

Used for tracking: No. Location data is used only for the core functionality of the app and is never used for behavioral tracking, analytics, or advertising

Storage: Stored locally in the app's database on your device; never transmitted or backed up to external servers

Photos (User-Selected)

Data collected: Photos you select from your Photo Library or capture using the device camera

Purpose: To associate visual records with your finds for personal reference and documentation

Linked to identity: No. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not associated with any user account or personal identifier. EXIF data (location, date, device info) from photos is not extracted or retained by the app

Used for tracking: No. Photos are used only for storage and display within the app, never for analytics or advertising purposes

Storage: Stored locally in the app's database on your device; never transmitted or backed up to external servers

Find Records

Data collected: Text descriptions, categories, tags, and metadata about items you catalog in the app

Purpose: To organize, search, and retrieve information about your finds

Linked to identity: No. All data is stored locally and is not associated with any user account

Used for tracking: No. Data is used only for app functionality

Storage: Stored locally in the app's database on your device

App Store Connect App Privacy Disclosure

In compliance with Apple's App Store Connect App Privacy requirements, here are the answers to the standard questions:

  • Does this app collect, use, or share user data? Yes. The app collects location and photo data.
  • Is the data linked to the user's identity? No. There is no user account system. All data is stored locally on the device.
  • Is the data used for tracking purposes? No. The app does not use data for advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics.
  • Data practices:
    • Location (Precise): Collected with user permission to record find locations
    • Photos (User-Selected): Collected with user permission to associate images with finds
    • Data sharing: No data is shared with third parties
    • Data deletion: Users can delete data at any time by deleting individual finds or clearing the app
    • Data retention: Data is retained as long as the user chooses to keep it in the app

Permissions and User Control

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Location (When In Use): Requested to capture the geographic coordinates of your finds. You can grant or deny this permission in Settings.
  • Camera: Requested to allow you to take photos of your finds directly within the app. You can grant or deny this permission in Settings.
  • Photo Library: Requested to allow you to select existing photos from your device to add to your finds. You can grant or deny this permission in Settings.

You can manage these permissions at any time through your device's Settings application under Privacy.

Data Security and Local Storage

All data collected by Finds is stored exclusively on your device's local storage. The app does not:

  • Transmit data to remote servers
  • Use cloud backup services for private data
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or crash reporting that collects personal data
  • Display advertisements or use tracking for advertising purposes
  • Require user account creation or login

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app functionality or applicable laws. We will notify you of significant changes through the App Store or by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
