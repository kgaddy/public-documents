Privacy Policy

Finds — Last updated: May 4, 2026

Finds is a personal finds-logging app for recording the location, photos, and details of items you discover in the field. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and what is shared with third parties.

Data We Collect

Finds collects the following on your device:

  • Precise GPS location — used to record the geographic coordinates of finds and site markers. Location is only accessed when you actively log a find or view the map.
  • User-created content — find details (title, notes, tags, dates), photos you attach, and site information you enter manually.

How Data Is Stored

All your content — finds, sites, photos, and settings — is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. No account is required, and no find data is transmitted to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

Third-Party Services

Finds uses the following third-party SDK:

  • Mapbox Maps SDK — renders the interactive map used to place and view finds. Mapbox may collect device identifiers and location data to provide map tiles and improve its services. This data is governed by Mapbox's Privacy Policy .

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

Permissions

  • Location (Precise) — required to geo-tag finds and center the map on your current position. You can deny this permission; the app will still work but coordinates will not be recorded automatically.
  • Camera & Photo Library — used to attach photos to a find. Photos remain on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Finds is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
